LAVÉ

Pronounced lah-VAY. One-time cleans of any size. Name your price, cleaners bid. By APPealing Labs.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Support

LAVÉ is a marketplace for one-time cleans of any size, from a whole home to just the fridge, oven or a bathroom. Post the job free and name your price or use LAVÉ's price. Independent cleaners bid, you pick one, and you pay only after you pick. Your payment is held until the job is done. No contract and no weekly plan. Homeowners pay the job price plus a 5% service fee; cleaners keep 95%. Available anywhere in the US. Cleaners are independent and are not background-checked or insured by LAVÉ. You pay by card through Stripe. Forgotten Tasks™ reminders help you remember the cleaning tasks most people overlook. Cleaners find local cleaning jobs, set their own price and get paid through Stripe. You must be 18 or older.
