LAVÉ

Pronounced lah-VAY. The Precision Cleaning Marketplace by APPealing Labs.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Support

LAVÉ is a cleaning marketplace. Choose the cleaning tasks you need, post a cleaning request and set a target price; independent cleaners nearby bid, and you pick the one that works for you. You pay by card through Stripe, and your payment is held until the job is done. Forgotten Tasks™ reminders help you remember the cleaning tasks most people overlook. Cleaners find local cleaning jobs, set their own price and get paid through Stripe. You must be 18 or older.
