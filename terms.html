Version 2026.10.01 · Last updated October 1, 2026

LAVÉ is operated by APPealing Labs. By using the app you agree to these Terms.

LAVÉ connects property owners with independent cleaning professionals. We do not employ cleaners. Pros are independent contractors performing real-world services at physical properties.

The owner pays the agreed price by card through Stripe. LAVÉ holds the funds until the owner releases them, or until 24 hours after the pro marks the job complete, unless the owner opens a dispute (which keeps the funds held while APPealing Labs reviews). On release, the pro’s payout is sent to the pro’s Stripe account; pros must complete Stripe onboarding and identity verification. Owners may be refunded by the pro or, after a dispute review, by APPealing Labs.

Fees: 10% platform fee, deducted from the pro’s payout; owners pay the agreed price. Stripe card-processing costs are covered by the platform fee.

Payments are for physical services consumed outside the app, not digital content, and do not use Apple In-App Purchase.

Report and block tools are in Profile. Check-In Safeguard is a convenience notification tool, not emergency response. Call 911 for emergencies.

Delete your account from Profile → Delete account & data (this also closes your payout account; it is blocked while a payment is held or in dispute), or email HQ@appealinglabs.com.
