LAVÉ Support

LAVÉ (pronounced lah-VAY) is from APPealing Labs. LAVÉ is a marketplace for one-time cleans of any size, from a whole home to just the fridge, oven or a bathroom. Post the job free and name your price or use LAVÉ's price. Independent cleaners bid, you pick one, and you pay only after you pick. Your payment is held until the job is done. No contract and no weekly plan. Homeowners pay the job price plus a 5% service fee; cleaners keep 95%. Available anywhere in the US. Cleaners are independent and are not background-checked or insured by LAVÉ.

Email: HQ@appealinglabs.com

For account or data deletion, use Profile → Delete account & data in the app, then email us if you also used a connected backend account.

Safety: cleaners (called Pros in the app) are independent contractors and LAVÉ does not run background checks or verify credentials. If anything feels unsafe, leave and call 911. Report a user from their listing or Profile → Safety, or email us; we respond within 24 hours.

App Review / press: same email. Response target: one business day.
