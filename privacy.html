LAVÉ Privacy Policy

Version 2026.10.03 · Last updated October 3, 2026

APPealing Labs (“we”) operates LAVÉ, The Precision Cleaning Marketplace.

1. Data we collect

  • Identifiers you enter: name, optional email, optional phone.
  • Profile details: role (owner or pro), city, credentials, service zones, property notes.
  • User content: menus, bids, signatures, reports, before/after photos, vault notes.
  • Location: precise location only with When In Use permission (nearby jobs and Start Clean).
  • Device: push token if you allow notifications.
  • Payments: Stripe processes cards and Connect payouts. LAVÉ does not store full card numbers or CVV. Our payments server stores, per job: amount, fee, status, Stripe payment and transfer IDs, your job title, a random device-bound identifier (a hash of a secret kept in your iPhone Keychain; not your name or email), and for pros a Stripe connected-account ID and payout code. Stripe collects pros’ identity and bank details directly.
  • Diagnostics: no third-party analytics SDK in the current binary. Apple may collect crash data.

2. How we use data

To run the marketplace, Protected Funds, Forgotten Tasks™ reminders, Check-In Safeguard, and support. We do not sell personal data. We do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use App Tracking Transparency advertising.

3. Sharing

  • Stripe, Inc. — card payments, payouts and identity verification.
  • Our payments server hosting (Expo EAS Hosting) and database (Upstash Redis), as processors, for the payment records described above.
  • An emergency contact you designate — only when you tap Ping contact.
  • Law enforcement when required by law.

4. Location and photos

Location is When In Use only. Photos are used for job verification and disputes and stay on device unless a later backend upload is disclosed.

5. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you use Profile → Delete account & data or uninstall. Delete account & data also closes your payout account and payout code on our payments server (not possible while a payment is held or in dispute). Payment records are kept as required for tax, fraud and legal obligations, in de-identified form after deletion. Email HQ@appealinglabs.com to request access or correction. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

LAVÉ is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
