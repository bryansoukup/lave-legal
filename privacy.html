LAVÉ Privacy Policy

Version 2026.09.23 · Last updated September 23, 2026

APPealing Labs (“we”) operates LAVÉ, The Precision Cleaning Marketplace.

1. Data we collect

  • Identifiers you enter: name, optional email, optional phone.
  • Profile details: role (owner or pro), city, credentials, service zones, property notes.
  • User content: menus, bids, signatures, reports, before/after photos, vault notes.
  • Location: precise location only with When In Use permission (nearby jobs and Start Clean).
  • Device: push token if you allow notifications.
  • Payments: Stripe processes cards and Connect payouts. LAVÉ does not store full card numbers or CVV.
  • Diagnostics: no third-party analytics SDK in the current binary. Apple may collect crash data.

2. How we use data

To run the marketplace, Protected Funds, Forgotten Tasks™ reminders, Check-In Safeguard, and support. We do not sell personal data. We do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use App Tracking Transparency advertising.

3. Sharing

  • Stripe, Inc. — payments and payouts when enabled.
  • Infrastructure providers under contract if a backend is connected.
  • An emergency contact you designate — only when you tap Ping contact.
  • Law enforcement when required by law.

4. Location and photos

Location is When In Use only. Photos are used for job verification and disputes and stay on device unless a later backend upload is disclosed.

5. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you use Profile → Delete account & data or uninstall. Email HQ@appealinglabs.com to request access, correction, or deletion of any server copy. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

LAVÉ is not directed to children under 13.
